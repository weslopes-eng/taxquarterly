TaxQuarterly Partner Program

For CPAs, EAs, bookkeepers, bloggers and communities that help US freelancers and 1099 workers with quarterly estimated taxes.

What partners get

  • 30% recurring commission on every sale you refer, for 12 months from each customer's first payment (monthly US$6 or yearly US$49 plans).
  • A personal discount code giving your audience or clients 10% off Pro.
  • Free Pro for you, so you can try everything you recommend.
  • Monthly payouts via PayPal or Wise, with a simple statement of the sales attributed to your code.

How it works

  1. Email us and we create your code (for example, YOURNAME10).
  2. Share the code wherever it fits: client emails, a resources page, a newsletter or a community post.
  3. Each month we tally the sales made with your code, net of refunds, and pay 30%.

What TaxQuarterly is

A planner that estimates the four federal 1040-ES payments plus state tax for 11 states, saves history, exports calendar reminders and prints a PDF summary. The core estimator is free. It is an estimating tool, not tax advice, and it does not replace a professional.
