Quarterly Estimated Tax Planner

For US freelancers & 1099 workers — tax year 2026. Free, no login, nothing leaves your browser.

What it does: enter your self-employment profit and other income, and the planner estimates your 2026 federal income tax plus self-employment tax, how much of each payment to set aside (%), the IRS safe-harbor amount based on last year's return, and the amount and due date of each quarterly Form 1040-ES payment. It is a calculator: it does not file or pay anything with the IRS and does not replace a tax professional.
